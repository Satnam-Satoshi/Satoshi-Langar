## Before publishing an invitation

- [ ] Host and backup accept responsibility.
- [ ] Venue, capacity, local safety and accessibility are reviewed.
- [ ] A meal event has separate kitchen/food-safety approval.
- [ ] Any resources/costs are confirmed.
- [ ] Photography is optional and consent is clear.
- [ ] Date, timezone, cancellation and concerns contact are correct.
- [ ] The host approves the exact public invitation.

## Public-safe proposal

Share region, proposed format, your contribution and permission status. Do not publish a private home address, participant list or private contacts. A proposal is not a booking or confirmed meetup. Preserve the draft privately until the relevant human approvals exist.
