## Start in 30 minutes

Choose one small planning contribution: understand a local need, review access, prepare a volunteer-role outline, or improve a consent-safe checklist. Do not announce a kitchen or recruit guests before the local human organizer approves it.

## Before any invitation

- [ ] Local organizer and backup have accepted responsibility.
- [ ] Venue, food safety, dietary needs, accessibility and safeguarding have appropriate local review.
- [ ] Resources are confirmed; planning does not authorize purchases or fundraising.
- [ ] Participation does not require a wallet, belief, payment, photo or proof of poverty.
- [ ] A private concerns route and cancellation plan work.
- [ ] The host has approved the exact public invitation.

## A safe public proposal

Share only the region, need, your proposed task, permission status, outstanding questions and help requested. Leave guest identities, private locations, phone numbers, medical/dietary details about individuals and private receipts out of GitHub.

Completing this brief does not schedule an event or appoint a steward. Keep it private or submit a redacted proposal yourself through the program page.
