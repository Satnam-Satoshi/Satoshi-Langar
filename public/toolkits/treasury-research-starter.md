## Check before sharing

- [ ] I distinguish retrieval time from the date the figure describes.
- [ ] Missing data is marked unavailable, not zero.
- [ ] Holdings, ownership, custody, AUM, flows and valuation are not silently mixed.
- [ ] Any mNAV calculation defines its numerator, denominator and timing; missing inputs stop the calculation.
- [ ] The source is public and may be cited.
- [ ] No personal wallet balances, credentials or account records appear.
