EDITOR’S NOTE

Institutional access needs precise evidence.

Afternoon revision, November 9 • Original desk cutoff: approximately 10:00 ET.

Litecoin’s institutional story has several separate parts: a listed investment product, company-held coins, proposed reserve-backed tokens and software still in testing. Their progress should be measured separately. A balance is not a fund flow, a memorandum is not an allocation, and a network announcement is not a completed integration.

The Register’s afternoon dataset totals 16,240,206.4928 LTC across 98 records. It combines different reporting dates and may double-count coins held through funds and custodians. Its Lite Strategy entry still uses July 17 data; the company’s newer September 30 filing reports 751,798 LTC across digital assets and a receivable category. The two records are explicitly reconciled on page 13. S03 / S25

Issuer data sharpen the investment-product picture. Canary’s holdings table is dated November 9, while its pricing panel remains November 6. Grayscale’s issuer identifies LTCN as an OTC trust. Neither should be described using an assumed ticker conversion or an unverified same-day flow. Page 28 keeps those definitions and dates visible. S24 / S34

Canton’s completed institutional pilot and the SEC staff’s Canton Coin response are meaningful context, but neither establishes that cLTC has launched. Greywick still labels cLTC as in build. LitVM’s documentation likewise separates its operational testnet from an unfinished native-LTC route and infrastructure migration. S26 / S27 / S30 / S28

Reading the clocks. This afternoon update refreshes Register data, investment-product evidence and ecosystem reporting. The morning price tape, issuer/company desks and project-release record retain their stated clocks. Morpho remains the corrected 15:41 UTC API snapshot. November 9 Bitcoin ETF constituent cells are still missing at this review, so the latest complete session remains November 6.
