# Satoshi Langar: first pilot planning template

**Status: planning template. No event or operating service is announced by this document.**

Start with a small real need and a human organizer. The organizer must establish local responsibilities and appropriate safety review before public invitations, participant intake or food/service delivery. This template is not a substitute for that review.

## The smallest useful plan

| Question | Complete before approval |
|---|---|
| Need | What community need has a human organizer confirmed? |
| Scope | What small action will be delivered, and what is outside scope? |
| Human owner | Who is accountable, and who can stop the pilot if they are unavailable? |
| Local review | Who will review food safety, safeguarding, venue permissions and relevant requirements? |
| Access | How can people participate regardless of language, disability, belief or crypto knowledge? |
| Consent | What is optional, what will be public and how can someone decline publicity? |
| Data | What minimum information is necessary, who may see it, and when will it be removed? |
| Resources | What is already available, and what requires separate approval? |
| Stop conditions | What problem means pause or stop, and who communicates that decision? |
| Learning | What useful outcome and feedback will be recorded without identifying recipients? |

## Keep dignity central

Service must not depend on religious participation, wallet ownership, a token balance or permission to photograph someone. Public planning should avoid beneficiary names, exact private locations and unnecessary personal data. Aggregate notes and a description of what was learned may be enough; publicity is not proof of service.

This repository currently has no approved donation collection, community pool or automated payment/reward mechanism. Planning a pilot does not authorize collecting money or using participants' stories in promotion.

## A short learning report, after an approved pilot

- Approved scope and accountable human organizer.
- What happened, with uncertainty and deviations noted.
- What participants chose to share, summarized with consent.
- What was useful and what should change.
- Incidents and follow-up through the appropriate private route.
- Whether to repeat, change or stop, and who made that decision.
