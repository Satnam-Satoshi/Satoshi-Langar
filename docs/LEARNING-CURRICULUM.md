# Sikh Bitcoin learning curriculum

Version: 2026-10-01. Status: implementation complete for an educational release; independent subject-matter and community review pending. AI-assisted original writing; no accredited course, institutional endorsement, human-review certification, wallet service or financial advice is claimed.

## Mission and audience

Sikh Bitcoin is Satnam Satoshi’s inclusive community learning program. The name draws on the sense of a learner while respecting Sikh as a living religious identity. It does not equate Sikh doctrine with Bitcoin or claim to speak for Sikh institutions. The World Sikh Organization’s explanation is linked in the course directory: https://www.worldsikh.org/sikh_faith.

Three complete courses provide 21 lessons each. Every lesson contains two learning outcomes, original explanatory sections, a paper exercise with a worked answer, at least two self-check questions with explanations, a takeaway and primary references. The original three starter URLs remain valid. Learners need no wallet, purchase, account, deposit or prior religious affiliation. All technical and financial scenarios are fictional learning exercises.

The curriculum emphasizes a distinction between proof-of-work consensus, application records and human evidence of service. A community grant is a transfer from an authorized budget, not newly issued bitcoin. Native BTC and Lightning are distinguished from token representations, third-party claims and lending contracts. No lesson proposes an individual trade, leverage level or real allocation. Completing the material gives no agent authority to operate a treasury.

## Learning paths and acceptance standard

1. **Bitcoin Foundations — beginner.** Explain money units, custody, payments and common risks; read news critically; complete a fictional artist-payment capstone. About four hours including practice. No prior knowledge required.
2. **Bitcoin Deep Dive — advanced.** For technical learners, builders and Bitcoin maxis: understand transactions, consensus, wallet standards, nodes and Lightning while separating policy from consensus. About five hours including practice. Foundations-level confidence recommended, not enforced.
3. **Sovereignty & Self-Custody — expert.** Design a threat model, recovery and human-accountable operations, then compare native BTC/Lightning with wrapped assets, USDC, Morpho and cross-chain dependencies. About five hours including practice. Deep Dive-level confidence recommended. A defensible decision not to deploy is a valid capstone outcome.

Completion is self-reported practice. Quizzes use native HTML details for answer reveal; answers are never sent to a server or stored. There is no fabricated exam result, score, certificate or professional qualification. The target of roughly 250–450 words applies to the full learning unit including exercises and explanations; the preserved starter lessons are longer. Automated content checks establish structure and sequence, not pedagogical correctness.

## Local progress: exact behavior

`app/sikh-bitcoin/components/LearningProgress.tsx` renders accessible HTML and a fallback explanation. `public/scripts/learning.js` progressively enhances pages marked `data-learning-progress`. The portable exporter is responsible for loading this trusted external script; no Next hydration or third-party library is needed. All reading, sources and quiz answers work without JavaScript.

- A learner can mark the current lesson complete or incomplete. Course lists show matching completion labels and a count/meter.
- Each course uses a separate localStorage key: `satnam-satoshi-learning-v1:<courseId>`. Stored fields are version, completed known slugs and update time. No name, wallet, quiz response or financial data is stored.
- Storage is scoped to this browser and origin. Another device, private browsing session or IPFS gateway may show different progress. Clearing site data can remove it. There is no cloud sync, authentication, cross-origin tracking or network request.
- Browser-storage failures are reported and marks can remain temporary in memory. Malformed stored data is handled without preventing lesson reading. Unknown lesson slugs are filtered out.
- Reset requires an inline confirmation and affects only the current course. Export downloads a JSON learning record; no import feature is claimed. The export states that completion is self-reported and is not a credential.
- Storage events refresh another open tab’s view. Simultaneous writes across tabs are not a transactional database; the tracker is a convenience, not a permanent academic record.
- Lesson navigation uses normal server-rendered hrefs. The script creates no course URLs, which preserves portable export and IPFS subpaths. Data attributes contain only lesson slugs/titles, not root-relative navigation targets.

## Evidence and review scope

Primary sources were inspected during the September 30–October 1 research session. References at lesson footers identify the governing technical material; classroom scenarios, arithmetic, review prompts and architectural recommendations are original educational synthesis. Older developer guides and BIPs are used for their documented concepts, not as a guarantee of current wallet support or implementation policy. Actual deployment parameters, asset addresses, market liquidity, provider eligibility and software behavior must be reverified before any real integration.

Material checks included BIP 141 weight/virtual-size rounding, BIP 32 public derivation privacy, BIP 39 passphrase behavior, BIP 174 workflow roles, BIP 380 recovery metadata, BIP 340/341 spend-path caveats, BOLT 11 expiry and Lightning channel-state recovery. Lending lessons use Morpho’s documented five market parameters, oracle/LTV model, variable-rate and vault risks. The numerical LLTV example is explicitly fictional; no configured market is recommended or certified. Circle reserve disclosure is separated from eligibility and terms, including issuer restrictions. Arc’s permissioned Malachite validator model and Base’s L1 derivation are described separately from Bitcoin proof of work.

These pages do not provide liquidation alerts or track anyone’s positions or debt. A market-data capability monitor elsewhere in the project does not change that limitation. No planning budget is represented as deposited, invested or managed.

## Agent governance for this contribution

- **Mission:** provide understandable, source-linked education that helps humans verify claims and serve their communities.
- **Responsibilities:** research, draft, implement within education scope, preserve URLs and test structural behavior; label uncertainty and limitations.
- **Knowledge sources:** primary protocol specifications, official technical documentation and the cited Sikh institution explanation; no unsourced live-market claims.
- **Permissions:** local education data/pages, the local-progress script and this documentation. No wallet connection, signing, transfers, financial-account creation or custody. Publication is handled under the founder’s release authorization by the coordinating workflow.
- **Escalation rules:** unresolved security, doctrinal, legal, technical or educational claims go to qualified human reviewers; do not substitute confidence or a generated citation.
- **Memory scope:** public curriculum and local implementation evidence only. Browser completion marks stay with the learner; no secret or personal finance data is requested.
- **Audit trail:** source URLs, curriculum version, review-status labels, repository diff and release validation evidence. Corrections should be versioned, not silently claimed as reviewed.
- **Human owner:** the Satnam Satoshi founder retains mission, publication, ethics and treasury authority.
- **Emergency stop:** stop delegated work on founder instruction; agents acquire no extra authority during an incident.
