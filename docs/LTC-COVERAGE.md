## What remains data work

Topic completeness is not numerical completeness. The accepted daily registry still has ten sources. It yields two single-venue BTC/LTC last trades, IBIT issuer holdings/shares and dated Bitcoin Core/Litecoin Core/LND release records; other endpoints record availability only. No flow series, mNAV calculation, current Morpho rate/liquidity, cbBTC/cbLTC reserves, USDC/EURC reserve totals, independent network telemetry, XLM/DOGE/PEPE quotes or automated SEC/CFTC headline feed is asserted by this release.

Future adapters must establish exact entity/chain identity, units, precision, cutoff, parser fixtures, source rights and stale/failure behavior. Do not treat holdings changes as flows, tracker additions as purchases, nominal rates as guaranteed returns, or asset tickers as contract identities. A current website link is not an adapter. CFTC’s changed rulemaking/comment destinations and differing agency document types must be preserved before an automated policy feed is accepted.

## Reference discrepancies retained

- Page 8 says twenty Bitcoin companies but actually lists 21; page 27 explains the 21st row.
- Page 11 contains 20 Litecoin product rows, including mixed product types and zero displays; it is not a table of 21 Litecoin ETFs.
- Page 12 contains 11 Litecoin companies. The Lite Strategy per-share checkpoint is historical, not current.
- Page 28 lists S01–S26 despite a footer saying S01–S24.
- The reference’s mNAV figures lack synchronized inputs and conflict with another issuer figure. No current multiple is calculated from them.
- The reference’s displayed cbLTC liquidity exceeds its displayed market size. The conflict is preserved; it is not executable available liquidity.
- The PDF’s 09:00 delivery/hourly-watch claims belong to a separate prior workflow. This site uses the recorded host-dependent 10:00 America/New_York local publication task; cloud remains inactive.
- EURC is newly requested scope, distinct from USDC and correctly spelled EURC. Arc’s current permissioned consensus is distinct from Bitcoin proof of work. cbBTC, cbLTC, cirBTC and native coins retain separate identities.
