# One community, connected projects

Satnam Satoshi is the umbrella. Each initiative should support useful human outcomes and have a named steward before operating. This map describes intent and current scope as of September 30, 2026.

## Satoshi Langar — service

**Purpose:** help communities plan and coordinate food and practical service with dignity.

**Today:** concept, public pages and a pilot planning template. A live meal program, beneficiary count, payment system and service-reward scheme have not been established here.

**Next useful outcome:** one reviewed [pilot plan](COMMUNITY-PILOT.md), with a human organizer, local safety review, minimal data and stop conditions. Help through [issue #50](https://github.com/Satnam-Satoshi/Satoshi-Langar/issues/50).

## Kalakar.x — creativity

**Purpose:** give artists, designers, writers and makers ways to contribute to the community and share work with permission and attribution.

**Today:** program discovery and a public story page. No marketplace or royalty/payment mechanism is claimed.

**Next useful outcome:** a small creative brief with an accountable curator, asset provenance, contributor permission and explicit reuse terms.

## Agent Sangat — collaboration

**Purpose:** make AI useful to human contributors through bounded, understandable tasks.

**Today:** governance rules and an [agent brief](AGENT-CONTRIBUTION.md). There is no open agent endpoint, self-authorizing registry or autonomous executive system.

**Next useful outcome:** one public, source-backed contribution reviewed by a named human, with permissions expired or revoked afterward. AI Satoshi Ma is a project assistance identity, not a legal officer or treasury signatory.

## Lunch Time Conversations — learning

**Purpose:** create accessible conversations, explanations and research with citations and visible corrections.

**Today:** editorial designs, historical work and prototype pages. A reliable automated publishing schedule is not established by this repository.

**Next useful outcome:** one human-reviewed edition that a reader can trace to its sources and correction record.

## Treasury Intelligence — research

**Purpose:** explore transparent public-data research and accounting methods, with Bitcoin/sats as a reference.

**Today:** research plans and separately reviewed prototypes, including [PR #45](https://github.com/Satnam-Satoshi/Satoshi-Langar/pull/45). Arc/Base/Morpho experiments are separate from the public community site. Positions and debt are not monitored by the current read-only capability monitor; no funds are managed.

**Next useful outcome:** clear data provenance, freshness, limitations and accounting reconciliation before any proposal for expanded capabilities. Community onboarding never depends on a deposit or borrowing.

## Crypto Kitty — mutual-aid research

**Purpose:** examine community savings circles, cooperation and mutual aid as ideas that could support people.

**Today:** research only. There is no operating chit fund, deposit intake, pool, token or promised yield.

**Next useful outcome:** a plain-language comparison of possible models, risks, responsibilities and professional-review needs. Research does not authorize custody, fundraising or an investment product.
