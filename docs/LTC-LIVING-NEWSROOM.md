# LTC: a living newsroom with a durable memory

Operating plan · October 5, 2026 · Human owner: Satnam Satoshi founder. AI Satoshi Ma coordinates editorial work; this is not a claim of a staffed newsroom, legal office or independent human verification.

## What success means

Make LTC useful enough that readers choose to return: explain Litecoin and Bitcoin accurately, connect news to learning, make primary evidence easy to inspect, and preserve the story. Search visibility is measured evidence, not a promise to rank first for every Litecoin query. No purchased followers, mass tagging, copied article farms or fabricated freshness.

## A reader's path

1. **Discover:** a clearly titled source briefing, shared edition, explanatory search result or community recommendation.
2. **Understand:** read the plain-language significance, exact dates, source classification and limitations.
3. **Go deeper:** follow the relevant fieldbook, MWEB/mining/LitVM desk, self-custody resource or structured lesson.
4. **Participate:** use the community guide, make a guest contribution plan, propose a public source/correction, or choose Google member sign-in.
5. **Return:** follow RSS and the dated issue library. Membership is not consent to marketing; email subscriptions remain disabled until delivery and unsubscribe checks pass.

Community Wire at `/conversations/community/` connects four complementary layers: current accepted project records, dated editorial reading selections, the founder's requested 13-account directory, and immutable source-check pages. X profile links are source-discovery links; they do not mean those accounts have been followed, endorsed LTC, or joined its team. Current ownership is not verified for every requested handle. X timeline ingestion is not connected.

## Editorial work and boundaries

| Role | Responsibility | Evidence and escalation |
|---|---|---|
| Source desk | Collect only accepted exact public endpoints; prepare candidate links from project pages | Keep response hashes, real collection times and failures. No blocked-source bypass or invented observations. |
| Evidence editor | Identify speaker, publication/event date, source class and material claim | A proposal is not a launch; a statement is not an independent test. Seek primary records for substantive claims. |
| Learning editor | Explain why a story matters and offer a useful next lesson | Preserve original research dates; do not turn an old feature into today's reporting. |
| Design/accessibility reviewer | Check phone/desktop reading, headings, navigation, artwork meaning | Decorative art is not a data chart. Publication dates and gaps stay legible. |
| Release archivist | Test, build, preserve Git source, verify candidate, promote, verify both domains | No release on failed checks, unexpected head, paused policy or changed implementation. Keep rollback and private receipts. |
| Search steward | Maintain distinct metadata, canonical URLs, crawlable links and sitemaps | Search Console verification and measurement remain pending; never claim indexing from a valid sitemap alone. |

These are workflow responsibilities, not always-running independent employees. The existing local schedule and authenticated services determine availability. The founder retains ownership, policy, expenditure and emergency stop authority.

A community item earns publication when identity and original date are checked, it contains useful evidence or a clearly labeled perspective, it adds something beyond repetition, and its summary links into the knowledge archive. Material political analysis, allegations, private narratives and substantial corrections need the relevant human review. Original public source summaries may be published under the founder's standing direction after checking. No imitation interviews or invented participation.

## Daily and weekly rhythm

- **10:00 America/New_York target:** the existing local publisher collects approved financial/network/policy sources and the two fixed community sources. It prepares the daily issue and a fresh source-check archive record, runs acceptance/build/browser checks and verifies public publication. Same-day edition repeats remain no-ops. A missed run leaves the last dated issue visible.
- **Existing 10:45 / 14:45 / 18:45 social-policy checks:** check accepted SEC/CFTC sources and prepare bounded original social copy. Actual channel publishing depends on verified supported connections and current social controls. Do not promise posting from a prepared queue or a connected account alone.
- **Daily community candidates:** inspect a small, useful selection of original public project sources when accessible. Keep unverified tweets as leads. Record source URL, original date, first seen, claim class, evidence, limitations and why a reader benefits. No website scraping to simulate an X API connection.
- **Weekly review target:** select one substantial knowledge gap, review corrections and broken links, inspect verified search data if access exists, and improve one existing article before expanding volume. This is a planning cadence, not a separately activated scheduler.

## Preservation

Daily editions and corrections keep existing immutable revision IDs and RSS events. Each community check is separately saved as `content/ltc-community/archive/YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ.json`; it gets a permanent public route, downloadable JSON and a record hash. The latest pointer must match the newest archived record. Old checks are validated against their saved clock, not rewritten or made current. A failed source remains a dated gap. Hashes identify bytes; they are not source signatures or truth guarantees.

The initial archive starts with a previously accepted October 5 check and the new evening check. It does not pretend to reconstruct checks that were never saved. Curated reading selections have their own selection date and original source dates; routine daily runs cannot edit their prose, directory identities or implementation.
