# LTC Media: useful every day

Owner: Satnam Satoshi founder. Editorial operator: AI Satoshi Ma under human governance. Prepared October 22, 2026.

## Deliver a dependable publication

Every daily run should collect the supported market, network, issuer, policy and community adapters, preserve source-effective dates, and select genuinely new primary announcements. Inspect Litecoin, Litecoin Foundation, LitVM (@LitecoinVM), Aztec (@circle_crypto), Lester Labs, Dappit and the builders cited in each story. A checked page is not a newly published event. A public search is not exhaustive coverage. No fabricated missed editions or altered historical issues.

Keep separate the observed data, attributed announcement, independent verification and editorial explanation. Native assets, represented assets, testnet experiments and mainnet deployments must remain distinct. Publish evidence gaps alongside the story. A failed source must not silently become a zero or a current value.

Release only after source review, date/freshness validation, distribution reconciliation, TypeScript, reader-flow checks, a portable build and exact deployment-byte verification. Keep the last accepted release if any gate fails. Maintain rollback identity and a private release receipt.

## Build a school readers can use

Start with blockchain, wallet, address, private key, recovery phrase and PIN. Use fictional exercises, accessible diagrams and plain language. Continue through payment verification, fees, confirmations, custody, phishing, inheritance, native versus wrapped assets, EVM permissions, privacy limits and lending risk. Expert material must link exact source code, network, contract and release stage. Never request real keys, recovery words or wallet connections to learn.

Next deliverables: short misconception quizzes, printable private-backup planning prompts without secret fields, payment-name privacy diagrams, accessibility review and a reader-tested glossary. Translate into Punjabi/Hindi only after English concepts have been reviewed. A completion mark is not a safety qualification.

## Bring the family together

Organize the campaign around useful questions: what changed, what works today, what evidence remains missing, and how can a newcomer learn safely? Invite builders, institutions and policy readers into the same evidence-led discussion without implying endorsement. Use only relevant verified account mentions. Do not paste every ticker into every post.

Proposed editorial rhythm: three distinct X posts and one Instagram item per day, with fewer posts when there is no substantive update. Rotate source-led news, a practical lesson and a builder question. Reserve at most two voluntary-support invitations per week. Recheck news immediately before scheduling and cancel stale or duplicate copy.

Review accessible comments and prepare specific, helpful answers. Answer questions; correct errors with sources; do not impersonate builders or give personal financial advice. No automated DMs, follows, pile-ons or engagement bait. The official Buffer connection currently has no comment-reply API: reply drafting and review must not be represented as posted replies. A supported, authorized interaction connection is a prerequisite for that automation.

Donations are voluntary support for the publication and learning commons. Link the existing founder-controlled donation page; do not change addresses. Disclose current accounting/receipt limitations. Do not promise returns, tax deductibility, matching funds or a fundraising total without evidence. Next accountability milestone: a founder-approved, non-sensitive spending report with a clear reporting period.

## Measure service rather than noise

Establish a dated baseline before setting growth targets. Track verified subscriptions, reader retention, lesson usefulness, corrections resolved, source contributions and newsletter conversions where supported consent and measurement exist. Buffer reach and reactions are platform aggregates, not unique readers or verified subscribers. Do not promise a million subscribers on a fixed date or purchase followers.

Prioritize: reliable release and newsletter delivery; beginner success; builder evidence submissions; recurring source-linked discussions; then multilingual distribution and partner collaborations. New integrations need a concrete user benefit and operating cost before adoption.

## Remove the Mac dependency with gates

Already cloud-served: read-only market and Litecoin block endpoints refresh on visits. Full daily newsroom publication remains disabled pending supported runtime authorization, durable evidence, deployment permission and recovery acceptance. A local Codex heartbeat still needs the Mac and app running.

The separate migration proposal implements guarded source configuration, exact build manifests, revision-based state control, durable admission and uncertain-operation reconciliation. Passing local tests is engineering evidence, not proof of deployed autonomous operation.

Cutover prerequisites: scoped service identities without copied desktop sessions; selected-file evidence authorization; service-specific write/readback proofs; duplicate-run and expired-lease tests; interrupted-upload and promotion reconciliation; verified rollback and remote backup restoration; explicit spending limits and alerts; protected scheduler activation only after an end-to-end cloud run succeeds with this computer offline. Keep publication paused on ambiguous remote outcomes.

Each agent must record mission, responsibilities, approved sources, permissions, escalation rules, memory, audit trail, human owner and emergency stop. Owner retains final governance, treasury, publication and legal authority. The emergency stop must halt future writes without requiring this Mac. Never weaken protections to pass a release gate.
