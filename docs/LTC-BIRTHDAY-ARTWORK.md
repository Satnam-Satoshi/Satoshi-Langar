# Proof of Birthday — revision 2 artwork and art direction

Created for the founder-requested visual redesign of the 84-page Litecoin fifteenth-anniversary special. This supersedes neither the original archive nor its images; the new files are separate revision-2 candidates for the lead to integrate.

## Editorial direction

**Silver anniversary, with a wink.** Use sculptural wit on the covers, warm ivory reading pages, electric cobalt chapter markers and restrained silver details. The front asks the reader to smile at a literal birthday for a public ledger; the back leaves a seat for a future contributor. The title is typeset by the publication, never baked into art.

Suggested palette: obsidian `#08090c`, cobalt `#123bd6`, warm ivory `#f5f0e5`, silver `#c3c8cf`, charcoal `#252932`. Headlines should be high-contrast editorial serif with decisive scale; source labels use a compact clear sans serif. Large folios, thin rules and deliberate margins carry continuity. Avoid bevelled UI cards, universal rounded corners, decorative sparkles and gradients behind body text.

Use at least six page compositions: image-led opener, wide diagram and short columns, documentary specimen with annotation, quote/portrait-free profile, data notebook with methods, and quiet closing essay. Every page should have a story-specific visual, but that visual may be a careful timeline, exact equation, relationship diagram, map of responsibilities or typographic specimen. Do not repeat a generic network graphic across topics. Decorative art is never a data chart. Quantitative 84-block counts belong in deterministic layout assets.

Do not reuse either cover internally at full size. The four landscape artworks should be chapter punctuation, not a background on dozens of pages. Preserve substantial image-free space around explanations. Cover and back source artwork is 1024×1536; interiors are 1536×1024. No resampling or claim of 300-dpi full-page print resolution is made. Keep typography and precise explanatory graphics vector-based in PDF output.
