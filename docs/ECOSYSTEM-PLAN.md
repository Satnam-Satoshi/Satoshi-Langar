## One useful community cycle

**Learn → meet → create → serve → verify → share what we learned.**

Sikh Bitcoin helps a newcomer understand the tools. Meetups introduce people to local stewards. Kalakar.x helps creators make useful work. Satoshi Langar turns a locally confirmed need into an accountable service plan. Agents assist each step with research, translation and coordination. Lunch Time Conversations is intended to publish original reporting and explain the results. Voluntary founder-directed BTC/LTC support is intended for open education and humanitarian seva; they never determine who may learn, eat or belong.

Our “Nakamoto standard” is the community's design language for open verification, voluntary participation, self-custody, transparent rules and Bitcoin as a monetary reference. It is not a separate protocol certification or a claim of endorsement by Satoshi Nakamoto. Bitcoin's peer-to-peer payment and proof-of-work foundations are described in the [whitepaper](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf). Teaching proof-of-work includes its tradeoffs and different network assumptions. Supporting study of Litecoin does not treat its security, asset or monetary policy as interchangeable with Bitcoin's.

## Sikh Bitcoin: learn with confidence

“Sikh Bitcoin” is the proposed education program name and an invitation to learn Bitcoin. It welcomes every faith and background. Its bilingual wording and cultural interpretation require a fluent community reviewer; it is not presented as a definitive translation or religious instruction.

Start with six short, openly readable modules. Each needs a written lesson, primary sources, a five-question knowledge check, an accessible exercise and a visible revision date. Use examples or a test environment; a learner never needs to buy bitcoin, disclose a seed phrase or connect a wallet to take a course.

| Module | Learning outcome | Exercise / completion evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Money, Bitcoin and sats | Explain a satoshi, scarcity and the difference between money and a promise | Explain the idea to a newcomer in plain language |
| 2. Verify, do not merely trust | Explain transactions, nodes and proof-of-work at a beginner level | Trace a public example without exposing personal addresses |
| 3. Keys, custody and recovery | Distinguish an address, key, recovery phrase and custodial account | Identify unsafe requests in a fictional recovery scenario |
| 4. Payments and Lightning | Understand invoices, fees, expiry and settlement states | Review a test invoice; no live payment required |
| 5. Create and serve | Understand creator terms, Langar consent and service records | Review a sample creative brief or service checklist |
| 6. Read the news critically | Distinguish fact, analysis, opinion, price and asset exposure | Trace one publication claim to its original source |

Keep instructor notes, subtitles/transcripts and correction history with the lesson. Begin English-first with a reviewed English–Punjabi glossary from [issue #49](https://github.com/Satnam-Satoshi/Satoshi-Langar/issues/49), then translate based on volunteer capacity. Completion is learning feedback, not a financial credential or a promise of rewards.

## Satoshi Langar: people serve; tools support

Langar is a Sikh institution of communal cooking, service and sharing that puts equality into practice. Volunteers prepare and serve food, and participation is open without discrimination. The [Sikh Coalition's explanation](https://www.sikhcoalition.org/about-sikhs/faq/) grounds this account. Our project should learn from existing gurdwaras and community kitchens; no affiliation with one is claimed. A knowledgeable Sikh reviewer should check teaching materials before release.

A proposed local cycle:

1. **Listen:** a human organizer confirms a need with the local community.
2. **Plan:** steward completes the [pilot checklist](COMMUNITY-PILOT.md), names a backup, checks venue and food safety, and approves resources.
3. **Prepare:** volunteers choose tasks. Agents can draft schedules, accessible instructions and quantity estimates; a person checks allergies, quantities and operational decisions.
4. **Serve:** humans prepare, transport, welcome and share the meal. An agent cannot substitute for a trained person supervising food or safeguarding.
5. **Review:** two distinct human roles—task steward and reviewer—check a minimal completion record. For a small pilot, disclose when reviewer independence is limited.
6. **Learn:** publish aggregate, consented outcomes and corrections. Decide whether to repeat, change or stop.

No meal requires a wallet, religious participation, proof of poverty, social post or photograph. Private recipient details must not enter public issues, AI prompts or permanent archives.

### What “proof of service” means here

It is a proposed evidence-and-review process for useful work. It is **not a blockchain consensus mechanism**, a guarantee that a claim is true or a ranking of human worth. A cryptographic signature can associate a statement with a signer; it cannot by itself prove a meal was safely served.

The minimum record contains a task ID, approved purpose, human owner, role, date/window, deliverable, private evidence reference if needed, reviewer decision, consent scope, correction history and retention deadline. Publish only the safe summary. Do not publish recipients' names, faces, wallet histories or precise sensitive locations.

Human service evidence might be a steward-confirmed completed shift and aggregate supply reconciliation. Agent service evidence might be an accepted translation, checked lesson or tested code change, with tool permissions and operator attribution. Duplicate records, self-approval and conflicts are flagged for human review. Disputes pause recognition or any associated grant until a different reviewer considers them.

### How a sevadar could receive support

A sevadar is a person offering service. The first release recognizes accepted contributions with consented attribution, learning and community thanks. This must not turn access to Langar into a commercial reward scheme.

A **future opt-in sats grant or expense reimbursement pilot** could work only after a human approves a funded budget, written eligibility, fixed caps, conflicts policy, accounting, jurisdiction-appropriate review and a way to contest mistakes. Its flow would be: agreed task → completed work → independent review → human payment approval → recipient supplies an invoice → authorized signer verifies and pays → restricted receipt and aggregate report. Agents can prepare a recommendation; they cannot award themselves funds or hold signing keys. No amount, payment date or entitlement is promised today. A grant to a human operator for an accepted agent contribution must be explicitly described as such; an AI is not a payee or legal officer.

## Kalakar.x: artists can build a direct payment relationship

The proposed first version is a curated directory and clear commission briefs. Artists retain control of their work and choose their own terms. No marketplace is operating yet.

The target flow is **discover → agree scope and rights → request the artist's invoice → verify payment status → deliver → acknowledge**. Record price unit, quote expiry, revisions, usage license, AI involvement, refund/dispute policy and delivery conditions before payment. A supporter pays the artist directly; Satnam Satoshi must not silently intermediate funds or deduct an undisclosed fee. Optional gifts to Langar require a separate explicit choice.

Evaluate self-hosted [BTCPay Server](https://docs.btcpayserver.org/Guide/) for Bitcoin on-chain and Lightning invoicing. Invoice generation, expiry, settlement, duplicate callbacks and refund handling need tests before a live launch. Network fees and hosting still need accounting. First acceptance evidence is a completed test-environment commission with verified rights and a human-reviewed support workflow. A live micro-pilot needs its own recipient verification and authorized signer; the roadmap is not permission to send a test payment.

## Lunch Time Conversations: an original daily magazine

Lunch Time Conversations is our original publication, not an assumed external news feed. The ambition is a daily edition combining political and policy news, blockchain analysis, Bitcoin/Litecoin market structure and institution-quality company reports. “Institution-quality” describes the evidence standard we aim to meet, not an accreditation or a claim of readiness. Existing [editorial decisions](ADR/ADR-0011-LUNCH-TIME-CONVERSATIONS-EDITORIAL-MODEL.md) and [provenance requirements](PUBLICATION-PROVENANCE.md) remain relevant.

Every item is visibly **news**, **analysis** or **opinion**. News reports attributable facts; analysis explains methods and assumptions; opinion is signed and separate. Corrections remain visible. AI-assisted drafts require an accountable editor. No automated draft becomes a published edition because a clock fired.

| Desk | Proposed sources and fields | Rule before publication |
|---|---|---|
| Politics and policy | Original government releases, legislation, regulator orders, court records, direct statements and attributable interviews | Separate what a source says from what has been independently verified; include context and meaningful competing evidence |
| Bitcoin ETF / ETP | Issuer documents and holdings, exchange records and official filings; instrument identity, jurisdiction, asset structure, shares, NAV, holdings and effective date | Do not label all ETPs as ETFs; distinguish observed figures from estimates and flows from price-driven AUM changes |
| Treasury-company analysis | Filings and issuer releases, such as [Strategy financial documents](https://www.strategy.com/financial-documents); holdings, debt, dilution, shares and valuation date | Show source cutoffs and conflicts; do not rank or recommend securities without a separately approved editorial scope |
| mNAV snapshots | Explicit numerator, denominator, currency, share basis and timestamps | Define the formula for that report; do not blend a gross-asset multiple with a net-asset multiple; show unavailable for missing inputs or an invalid denominator |
| Litecoin Register | Attributed snapshots of the external Litecoin Register secondary source, reconciled to issuer evidence with dates and confidence | Do not imply an official registry or complete wallet attribution; unknown is not zero |
| Community and technology | Accepted contributions, maintainer releases and consented local learning reports | No invented partnerships, event attendance, service counts or unreviewed security claims |

The SEC's [investor bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/ETPBulletinSeptember2024) distinguishes types of exchange-traded crypto exposure; use each instrument's own documents for current details. ETF/ETP holdings may have an earlier effective date than retrieval time. A report about Strategy is independent analysis, not association with the company.

Daily operation must earn its label: first appoint an editor and backup, complete three reviewed editions, then run a seven-day rehearsal with source-failure, stale-data and correction tests. A daily draft pipeline may help, but weekends, market holidays and missing data need explicit labels. Show “last published” and “data as of”; never manufacture a fresh issue or a number. Public RSS and an exportable archive are preferred distribution outputs once implemented.

| Candidate | Purpose | Required evidence before adoption |
|---|---|---|
| Bitcoin Core | Independent transaction verification and education | Named operator, maintenance budget, backup and verification runbook |
| BTCPay Server + Lightning | Creator invoices and future approved donation checkout | Test invoices, recipient verification, settlement monitoring, recovery and support tests |

| Nostr | Portable public identity and community updates | Human-controlled key recovery, relay policy, moderation and export exercise; protocol reference: [NIP-01](https://github.com/nostr-protocol/nips/blob/master/01.md) |

| Git + static site + IPFS copies | Reusable source and recoverable public content | Restore a source/content export on an independent host; verify links and releases |

These are candidates, not installed integrations or partnerships. Hosted services still have operators and dependencies. Do not claim full independence until a second person has performed a recovery without the original provider.
