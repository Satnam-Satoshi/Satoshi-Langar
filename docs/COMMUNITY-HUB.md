## Growth loop

1. A dated magazine story or useful educational resource leads to one clear next action.
2. A guest picks an interest and makes a small private plan.
3. The guest completes a lesson, source check, sketch, test note or local-host brief.
4. The person chooses whether to submit their own public proposal.
5. A human steward reviews scope, evidence and consent; the project records accepted work visibly.
6. With permission, an editor may turn a useful result into a sourced community story. Readers find the next useful step.

This is the intended operating loop. Human intake/moderation stewards and response schedules are still being established. Do not claim conversions, membership totals, automatic assignments, completed events or Foundation partnerships. Do not add advertising trackers to measure growth silently. Future aggregate analytics and referrals require a separate privacy decision.
