## One story, two clear identities

**Satnam Satoshi** is the open-source community project: people and responsibly governed AI learning, creating and serving together. Its purpose is useful participation—education, open tools, creativity and proposed service programs. Seva gives the work a human purpose. Joining the reading table does not require purchasing an asset or making a donation.

**LTC Magazine / Lunch Time Conversations** is its publication: original, illustrated reading about Litecoin, Bitcoin and open technology, with dated evidence. LTC is the publication name; it does not imply official representation of the Litecoin Foundation or any other project.

Use this longer introduction consistently:

> We are opening a reading table around Litecoin’s fifteenth anniversary. LTC Magazine follows the people, engineering choices and public records behind the network. Satnam Satoshi invites readers to turn curiosity into something useful: a checked source, a clearer lesson, a thoughtful piece of art or a small open-source contribution. Beginners belong here. So do people who know the code. Many hands. One humanity.

The relationship should be visible in account descriptions and website links: **“LTC Magazine — a Satnam Satoshi project.”** Keep Litecoin’s achievements and tradeoffs together. This is a community invitation, not an investment pitch, a prediction or an endorsement from the people covered.

## Participation without a dead end

Offer one next step at a time:

- **Reader:** Open a chapter and follow one citation.
- **Newcomer:** Try a lesson without signing in or connecting a wallet.
- **Experienced reader:** Identify a claim, link the dated primary evidence and explain the correction.
- **Builder, artist or AI contributor:** Prepare one bounded contribution, then intentionally submit it through the actual public project process. Human maintainers decide what is accepted.
- **Local service participant:** Read the Langar concept and propose a useful support task. No live kitchen operation, organizer agreement or reward is implied.

For a public correction, ask for the page URL, the exact disputed statement and a primary source. Keep private contact details and sensitive reports out of public GitHub issues. Discussion participants should opt in; the campaign does not authorize mass messages.

Use two topical hashtags at most on a short post. **#Litecoin** fits the anniversary; **#LTCMagazine** identifies this campaign; **#Bitcoin** fits an actual comparison or Bitcoin learning post. Avoid unrelated trending tags, investment language and manufactured “viral” claims. Hashtags do not imply affiliation.
