## Recommended first seller platform

**Fourthwall’s free plan** is a practical initial candidate for a small print-on-demand collection. Its official pricing lists a free storefront and published catalog base costs deducted from each sale, plus payment processing. This avoids committing to a bulk stock order. Actual costs, destination shipping and margins still need review. [Official pricing](https://fourthwall.com/pricing), checked October 4, 2026.

Keep the community site and editorial archive on their current hosting. Link verified product pages to the storefront after testing. This introduces a third-party commerce dependency; it does not make the shop decentralized. Keep artwork, product copy and approved specifications in this repository so the collection remains portable.

## Founder activation steps

1. Use an owner-controlled email to create or sign into a Fourthwall shop. Choose the free plan and confirm the account’s recovery details privately. Do not invent a legal trust or company status.
2. Check country eligibility and complete payout setup privately in **Settings → Billing and payouts → Set up your payouts**. Identity, tax and bank information belongs in the provider’s own secure flow, never in this repository or chat. [Official payout setup](https://help.fourthwall.com/frequently-asked-questions/payments-and-pricing/how-you-get-paid), checked October 4, 2026.
3. Select one actual product per concept. Confirm usable print areas, materials, size charts, shipping countries, base costs, processing and returns. Prepare suitable artwork before setting retail prices.
4. Approve the specific sample cost before placing any sample order. Review physical quality and product-page accuracy, including sizing and color limitations.
5. Establish a monitored customer-support address and record the seller identity and operating responsibility. Review published shipping and refund terms with the owner.
6. Supply the verified storefront/product URLs. Replace concept status with the actual reviewed product details, then test the complete buyer journey. Any paid test order needs separate approval. Open sales only after the founder accepts the final collection and commercial terms.
