# Code of Conduct

Satnam Satoshi is a global, mission-driven community. Participation must preserve dignity, safety, truthfulness, and voluntary cooperation.

## Expected conduct
- Treat people with respect regardless of background, identity, technical skill, wealth, geography, or worldview.
- Critique claims and systems without demeaning people.
- Cite evidence and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy, consent, and local law.
- Protect vulnerable people and humanitarian beneficiaries from exploitation or publicity pressure.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Keep financial, political, and religious persuasion separate from access to basic dignity and service.

## Unacceptable conduct
Harassment, threats, hate, doxxing, fraud, impersonation, coercion, manipulation of evidence, undisclosed paid promotion, unauthorized financial solicitation, or attempts to obtain private keys, credentials, or personal data.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or escalate incidents to the Human Founder or designated human steward. Serious security or legal matters should follow SECURITY.md and appropriate lawful channels.

The goal of enforcement is to protect people, preserve mission integrity, and keep contribution possible.
