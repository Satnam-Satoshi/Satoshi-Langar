### Lead — The treasury-company model enters a harder phase

Strategy sold 1,690 BTC for approximately $108.6–109 million in the latest reported week. Contemporary reporting describes this as part of a multi-week selling sequence and says proceeds are being used in part for preferred-stock repurchases. The editorial significance is not a one-day BTC price move; it is that the best-known corporate Bitcoin treasury strategy is now demonstrating that treasury companies can become sellers as capital-structure demands change.

**Confidence:** HIGH for the latest 1,690 BTC sale; MEDIUM for broader market interpretation.  
**Freshness:** 2 days or less at cutoff.  
**Desk note:** Do not extrapolate Strategy's actions to Bitcoin network fundamentals.

### Bitcoin / Nakamoto Standard — Separate the network from financial wrappers

Bitcoin remains the reference case for permissionless Proof of Work: consensus validation, monetary issuance and settlement are enforced by network rules rather than a corporate treasury manager. LTC will use the phrase **Nakamoto Standard** editorially for the institutional qualities worth measuring across PoW systems: independently verifiable rules, costly block production, open validation, credible monetary policy and resistance to unilateral control.

Bitcoin Core 31.0 was released April 19, 2026. The LTC desk should treat current node software, reproducible metrics and open review as infrastructure—not price narrative.

**Confidence:** HIGH.  
**Freshness:** Bitcoin Core release current as verified from Bitcoin Core's official site.

### Litecoin / MWEB — Security maintenance is the story

The official litecoin-project GitHub releases page currently exposes Litecoin Core v0.21.5.5 as its latest indexed release. Its release notes describe important MWEB consensus hardening, node reliability improvements, wallet/mining fixes and security updates, and strongly encourage upgrading. The release specifically adds validation and state-handling hardening around MWEB.

Community posts refer to a newer 0.21.5.6 maintenance release, but the canonical GitHub releases page available to this desk did not independently expose that release at cutoff. **LTC will not promote 0.21.5.6 as verified until a canonical project source is captured.**

MWEB should be explained as Litecoin's opt-in privacy/fungibility extension, not as a separate coin or investment product.

**Confidence:** HIGH for v0.21.5.5; UNVERIFIED for v0.21.5.6.  
**Freshness:** official release page checked at cutoff.

### Merged mining — Security relationships require exact language

Merged mining lets compatible Proof-of-Work chains reuse mining work without requiring miners to abandon the parent-chain work. LTC should distinguish **technical merged-mining compatibility**, **observed miner participation**, and **economic security claims**. No daily merged-mining percentages are published in this edition because the desk did not obtain a primary, timestamped dataset suitable for the cutoff.

**Confidence:** HIGH for mechanism; DATA WITHHELD for daily figures.

### Stellar / DTCC — Public-chain connectivity is real; 2027 is the operative date

DTCC and the Stellar Development Foundation announced plans for DTC's Tokenization Service to connect with the Stellar public blockchain. DTCC says DTC-tokenized assets are expected to become available on Stellar in the first half of 2027. DTCC separately reported successful processing of U.S. trades using DTC-tokenized assets in July 2026 as the broader service moves toward an October 2026 launch.

The correct institutional framing is **market-infrastructure connectivity**, not a claim that DTCC holds XLM or that tokenized securities automatically create material XLM investment demand.

**Confidence:** HIGH.  
**Freshness:** DTCC primary-source material through July 2026.

### Morpho / wrapped BTC — Utility comes with layered risk

Morpho supports permissionless isolated lending markets using cbBTC as collateral. Morpho's own launch material describes cbBTC as an ERC-20 representation backed 1:1 by BTC held by Coinbase. That architecture adds layers absent from native Bitcoin custody: issuer/custodian exposure, smart-contract risk, oracle risk, liquidation mechanics, chain/bridge assumptions where applicable, and market-specific parameters.

No APY, utilization, liquidation or cbBTC/cbLTC rate is printed today because the desk did not capture a primary timestamped market snapshot at cutoff. **No figure is better than a stale figure.**

**Confidence:** HIGH for architecture; DATA WITHHELD for daily market figures.
