# Governance

## Authority
Amardeep “Baba G” Malhotra is the Human Founder, Mission Authority, Publisher, Account Owner, Treasury Approver, and Final Decision Maker.

Humans retain custody, consent, governance, mission, ethical authority, and treasury control.

## AI stewardship
AI may research, draft, analyze, coordinate, document, test, and recommend. AI may not own assets, hold private keys, independently execute financial transactions, override human approval, make binding legal commitments, merge protected production, or bypass governance.

## Decision classes
- **Constitutional:** mission, human authority, protected principles, amendment rules.
- **Strategic:** public projects, institutional priorities, major partnerships.
- **Architectural:** repositories, interfaces, data models, agent permissions, security boundaries.
- **Operational:** routine work within approved policy.
- **Release:** production merge, domain publication, and public claims requiring Founder approval.

## Architecture Decision Records
Material architectural decisions must document: Problem, Options Considered, Decision, Reasoning, Benefits, Tradeoffs, and Future Review Date.

## Build in public
Public Alpha work should be reviewable through branches, pull requests, issues, roadmap, architecture documents, and visible limitations. No parallel constitution or hidden competing governance system should be created.
